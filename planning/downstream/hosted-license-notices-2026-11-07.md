as selectable, read-only native text. Prove that a normal Release app exposes
those exact shipped texts through Help and About, with genuine Copy and Find.
This supplies a bounded engineering prerequisite for #14/#16/#5; it does not
approve rights, publish corresponding source or certify a final signed package.

## Journey and invariants

normal external app launch, public CLI file-open and unchanged public
folder/sidebar/Find/editor/Bold/Undo journey first. Then:

1. Open Help → Open Source Licenses; require a real visible native window.
2. Require displayed product text to equal the complete canonical MIT file.
3. Select all and Copy through real keyboard input; require the clipboard to
   equal those exact bytes as decoded UTF-8, after a fresh clipboard clear.
4. Select Third-party notices; require full displayed text equality to the
   generated corpus, including its end, and actual keyboard Copy equality.
5. Open native Find through Cmd-F, enter Graphviz and invoke Find Next; retain
   actual screen/accessibility evidence and verify notices stay unchanged.
6. Close only the licences window, open About through the app menu, use its
   existing licences button, and require the real licence surface to return.
