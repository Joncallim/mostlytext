Memory sampler details qualify the earlier aggregate peaks. The Mermaid test-group peak was 2.48 GiB and contained many `swift-frontend` compiler processes; it is not renderer memory. Its newly seen WebKit helper peak was 267 MiB. D2's newly seen helpers peaked at 950 MiB, chiefly one WebContent process at 915 MiB; the 256-edge chain/star helpers peaked near 747/752 MiB. Graphviz's new helpers peaked at 150 MiB. `ps` sampled every 0.2 s; helper attribution is based on same-user new PIDs, not a strict process-ownership proof. Peaks may fall between samples and are not a validated memory ceiling.

The Graphviz `Aborted()` classifier addresses the real first hosted failure recorded on [issue #15](https://github.com/Joncallim/mostlytext/issues/15); it does not explain or repair WebKit's underlying abort. No original issue #15 historical-hang criterion is closed by this result.
